Templates (5 Stages)

Each stage has its own subject line, body, and personalization instructions. Use [PERSONALIZED] in the body where Claude should inject the personalized portion. Other placeholders supported: {{name}}, {{first_name}}, {{handle}}. Generate a sample to preview, then approve. Editing an approved template resets approval.

Attachments

Files attached to outgoing emails as real attachments (paperclip in Gmail, not inline).

Max 15MB per file. Currently apply to all 5 stages.

Assign Creators

Pull creators directly from a scrape job's matched results, or paste handles manually. The tool reads the creator's email from the contacts table (extracted during bio scraping).

Filter matched creators to only those whose match_cues include any of these.
